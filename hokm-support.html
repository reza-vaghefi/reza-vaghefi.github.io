Reporting a bug

The fastest way to report a problem is from inside the app: open Settings → Report a Bug. It opens an email already addressed to us with your app version, iOS version, and device model attached, so we don't have to ask for that separately.

If you'd rather write in directly, email reza.card.games@gmail.com and describe what happened — what you expected, what happened instead, and roughly when in the hand it occurred, if relevant.

How to play

Hokm is a 4-player, partnership trick-taking card game. You and your partner play as a team against the other two players. Each hand, one player becomes Hâkem and chooses the trump suit (Hokm) based on their first five cards. Follow suit when you can — the highest card of the suit led wins the trick, unless someone plays trump.

Win 7 of the 13 tricks in a hand to score a point. Sweep all 13 tricks for a Kot — worth 2 points if your team was Hâkem, or 3 if it wasn't. First team to 7 points wins the game.

FAQ

What does Autoplay do?

Turns on the same AI that plays for your opponents, just for your own hand — useful if you need to step away mid-hand. It turns itself back off once the hand ends.

Can I change the table color or card design?

Yes — open Settings from the top bar to pick a table color and one of two card back designs.

Is my game data private?

Yes. Hokm doesn't collect or transmit anything — your stats are stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does Hokm support online multiplayer?

Not yet — today it's you and an AI partner against two AI opponents. Online play is something we're considering for a future update.
