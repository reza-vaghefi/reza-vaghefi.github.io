Hokm!

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026 — v1.0

Short version: Hokm doesn't collect, transmit, or share anything. There are no accounts, no network requests, and no analytics or advertising SDKs anywhere in the app. The only data that exists is a small set of game statistics saved on your own device, which you can erase by deleting the app.

What we collect On your device Sharing Children Changes Contact
01

What we collect

Nothing. Hokm has no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts — there's nothing to identify you by in the first place. The app makes no network requests of any kind, so no gameplay data, device information, or usage activity is ever sent anywhere, by us or by anyone else.

There are no third-party SDKs in the app — no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.

02

What stays on your device

Hokm saves a small amount of information locally, using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). It never leaves your device:

  • Game statistics — lifetime totals like games and hands won or lost, time played, and how you did as Hâkem.
  • Your card-back preference — which design you last picked in Settings.

This information is tied to the app, not to you personally, and is erased automatically if you delete Hokm from your device.

03

Sharing & third parties

We don't share anything, because we don't collect anything to share. Hokm doesn't integrate with any third-party service, and none of the local statistics above are ever transmitted, sold, or made available to anyone.

04

Children's privacy

Hokm is suitable for a general audience, including children. Since the app collects no personal information from any user — of any age — there is nothing collected from children that would require parental consent.

05

Changes to this policy

If Hokm's data practices ever change — for example, if a future update adds an online feature — this page will be updated first, with a new effective date above, before that update ships.

06

Contact
