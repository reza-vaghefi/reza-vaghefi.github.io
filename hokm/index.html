Hokm!

The classic Persian trick-taking card game — sharp, fast, and full of strategy.

Download on theApp Store

Play a hand whenever you want

You and your AI partner against two AI opponents, each with their own play style. No sign-up, no accounts, no waiting for other players — just pick up and play.

Each hand, one player becomes Hâkem and calls trump. Follow suit, take tricks, and race your team to 7 points before the other side does.

♠️

Three AI skill levels

Opponents and your partner play with increasingly sharp heuristics — from a beginner bot up to one that reads the table and plays defensively.

📊

Track your progress

Lifetime stats on games and hands won, time played, and how you do as Hâkem — all saved right on your device.

🎨

Make it yours

Pick a table color and a card back design to match your taste.

⏱️

Pause and resume

Close the app mid-hand and come back later — Hokm picks up exactly where you left off.
